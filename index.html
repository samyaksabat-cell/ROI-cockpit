How the model works

Monthly assisted PRs = scope engineers × adoption share × pilot PRs per active engineer per normalized month × pilot assisted share.

Capacity hours = assisted PRs × assumed labor hours saved per PR. Capacity value = capacity hours × (annual loaded cost ÷ annual productive hours). Spend = adopted engineers × illustrative monthly cost.

Year-one totals sum all 12 months. ROI = (capacity value − spend) ÷ spend × 100. A normalized month is 365/12 days. Readiness, credit consumption, cycle time, and review turnaround never multiply the financial benefit.

Cumulative break-even is the first month with positive cumulative spend and cumulative capacity value at least equal to spend. It excludes unmodeled upfront investment.

What needs to be true

    Before a rollout decision

    1. Confirm comparable teams, measurement definitions, and PR attribution with the engineering sponsor.
    2. Validate assumed labor hours saved using a sampled time study, not elapsed cycle-time changes.
    3. Confirm adoption, commercial terms, productive hours, and excluded costs with finance.
    4. Agree a decision gate, a measurement owner, and the next observation period.
